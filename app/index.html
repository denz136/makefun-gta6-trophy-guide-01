Platinum Roadmap — Vice Got a Price!

Built from the official trophy descriptions and GTA V's platinum structure. GTA VI lists no online trophies, so the whole platinum can be earned solo. Roughly 60–80 hours by the estimates below.

Phase 1 Play the story — 14 trophies

  • Earn every story trophy naturally: Just Like Paradise through You Gotta Know When to Get Off, plus the mission trophies Trust, Break the Bank, Family Matters, DWNFALL, Hi-Jacked, Snow Storm.
  • Do NOT skip side content while playing — that feeds Phases 2–4 and 100% Completion (A Long Road).
  • Missable alert: Whip It Good-adjacent showroom stunt (Capital Offense) is best done as soon as Vice City opens in Chapter 3.

Phase 2 Side activities & mini-games — 16 trophies

  • Try every activity type once for Master of None; beat Cal (fishing), finish an arcade game (Stick Fiddler), go bowling (Let's Go Bowling), enjoy the Ocean Beach cocktail (In The Beginning…).
  • Complete all 6 stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) — they add up to Strangers & Freaks (8 strands across Leonida).
  • Partner-trophy pair: Hot Together (escape a 4-star wanted level together) and Retail Therapy (3 shops back-to-back with your partner).

Phase 3 Crime side jobs — 9 trophies

  • Sneakthief (undetected jewelry heist), A Classic Blitz Play (armored truck), Maximum Withdrawal (5 vaults), Pest Control (one illegal operation per gang), Challenging Tank (Rhino), Catch Me If You Can (VCIA departure).
  • Money trophies: Dime on the Dollar ($250k laundering) and Treat Yo Self ($100k shopping) — they come naturally while buying garages (Little Boxes).
  • On the Spot: 10 Scooter Bros registrations from the in-game phone app.

Phase 4 Collectibles & exploration — 8 trophies

  • Discover 100 vehicles (Whip It Good), WAiNK 100 locations (You WAiNKer!), 3 card sets (Don't Touch My Deck), meditate everywhere (Positive Mental Attitude), explore all Leonida (The Goodtime State).
  • Do these DURING the story so the 100% checklist never surprises you — A Long Road (gold, 100% Completion) closes here.

Phase 5 The grind — counters & cleanup — 8 trophies

  • Cunning Stunts (250 stunts), Top Fun (drone race every location), Earn Cash, Not Kudos (20 different races), The Shootist (gold in all 42 shooting-range challenges), One Last Thing (50 challenges).
  • Any leftover story-choice trophies (Actions Speak Louder, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Personal Best) — make a manual save before late-story choices.
  • Finally: Vice Got a Price! — the Platinum pops when the 54th trophy lands. 🏆

⚠ Missable trophies flagged in the dataset: Capital Offense (showroom), Actions Speak Louder, Bad Luck I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin — make manual saves before late-story branches and before one-off stunts.
